# Story Studio expansion

Twelve new original-fiction stories add 36 separately written reading editions. Each edition has a lead, three complete sections and a reflection prompt. The editions develop the same story at different levels rather than truncating a longer text.

| Story | Thinking skill | Suggested image family |
|---|---|---|
| A Bridge for a Beetle | Design for another user | robot-story |
| The Quiet Bench Club | Separate observation from assumed motives | library-story |
| The Envelope with No Name | Keep traceable records | rain-story |
| The Clock with No Batteries | Record a changing pattern | rain-story |
| The Kite with Two Tails | Change one feature and acknowledge other variables | robot-story |
| The Map You Could Hear | Represent information while protecting private details | library-story |
| A Recipe for Everyone | Test instructions with a new reader | chapati-story |
| The Borrowed Colour | Permission, attribution and collaboration | library-story |
| The Cup of Questions | Ask for clarification kindly | rain-story |
| The Last Piece Was a Question | State assumptions behind a pattern | chapati-story |
| The Saturday Repair Table | Recognise the limits of competence | library-story |
| Two Routes to the Same Place | Distinguish measures, assumptions and objectives | robot-story |

The imageKey values are suggestions for a related illustration family, not claims that the illustration depicts the exact characters or event. All characters and events are fictional. No generated scene should be represented as documentary evidence.

All practical activities include adult involvement where appropriate. Unknown seeds are not tasted; Sun observation avoids looking directly at the Sun; kite flying stays away from roads and wires; electrical repair is reserved for an appropriately qualified adult. Travel activity is a paper model rather than a route recommendation.

Validation: 12 unique IDs, three age editions each, exactly three sections per edition, complete reflection prompts, valid existing related-article IDs. Every 6–8 edition has at least 100 words, every 9–11 edition at least 180, and every 12–14 edition at least 250.
